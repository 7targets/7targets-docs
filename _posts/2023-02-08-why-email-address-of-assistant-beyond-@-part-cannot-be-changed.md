An Assistant’s email domain (the part after `@`) is fixed. If the Assistant uses a 7Targets-managed address and has no linked external CRM user, you can edit only the part before `@` in **Edit Assistant**. The full email address is fixed for Assistants that use a connected mailbox or are linked to an external CRM user.
